## Project outline

Each subject climbs three levels. Level 1 lives in my own notes; this site holds the Level 2 and Level 3 books.

| Subject | Level 1 (foundation) | Level 2 (intermediate) | Level 3 (advanced) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanics | Tipler & Mosca | [Taylor: Level 2 Classical Mechanics](level2-book/index.md) (12 chapters, 7 math) | Goldstein (planned) |
| E&M | Tipler & Mosca | [Griffiths: Level 2 Electricity and Magnetism](level2-em-book/index.md) (10 chapters, 3 math) | Jackson (planned) |
| Quantum | Tipler & Mosca (modern physics) | Griffiths QM (planned) | Sakurai (planned) |
| Thermo / stat mech | Tipler & Mosca | Schroeder (planned) | Pathria (planned) |
| Math, alongside | Stewart | Boas (with Stewart and Zill) | Arfken (with Zill) |
