title: Level 2 Classical Mechanics

Notes for the Level 2 (Taylor) rung of the mechanics ladder, with the Level 2 math alongside. Each mechanics topic matches a row of the textbook ladder workbook, and each math notebook covers the math those topics need. The explanations, examples, exercises and figures are original; chapter references to Taylor, Stewart, Boas, Zill and Arfken are approximate, as in the workbook.
