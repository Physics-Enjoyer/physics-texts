Notes for the Level 2 (Griffiths) rung of the E&M ladder, with the extra Level 2 math alongside. Each chapter matches a row of the textbook ladder workbook, including that row's example ladder. The explanations, examples, exercises and figures are original; chapter references to Griffiths, Tipler & Mosca, Jackson, Stewart, Boas, Zill and Arfken are approximate, as in the workbook.

Much of the math was already covered in the mechanics book, so the three math notebooks here cover only what is new for E&M. The table lists both.
