title: Level 2 Thermal and Statistical Physics
---

Notes for the Level 2 (Schroeder) rung of the thermo and stat mech ladder, with the extra Level 2 math alongside. Each chapter matches a row of the textbook ladder workbook, including that row's example ladder. The explanations, examples, exercises and figures are original; chapter references to Schroeder, Tipler & Mosca, Pathria, Stewart, Boas, Zill and Arfken are approximate, as in the workbook.

Topics 1–4 (Schroeder 1–4) can run alongside mechanics. Topics 5–8 (Schroeder 5–8) come after quantum topics 2 and 5, since they count quantum states.
